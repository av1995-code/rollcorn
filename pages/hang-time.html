Today's goal
 

Tap when the arrow is on the gold to punt. Then tap to rise, and hold to float.

Score
10 points for every second in the air, plus bonuses for corn, kicks through the uprights and close calls. You get three lives a game, and your score carries across them.
Slots
A punt earns a spin at 150, 400 and 800 points. A golden ear, halftime or beating your best score earns a golden spin, which always wins. Every ear you catch also feeds the jackpot.
Power-ups
The slots give them: one in your hand and up to two waiting. You can also catch one on the field when your hand is empty. Press the space bar, or tap the badge, to use the one in your hand.
Keys
Space or Enter punts. In the air, Enter or the up arrow taps and space uses your power-up. Down arrow, or a drag downward, dives. P pauses.

Hang Time is free. Corn and spins cannot be bought, and they have no cash value.

How it works
  • Tap, click or press the space bar to punt. Every tap after that lifts the ball. Stop tapping and it drops.
  • Fly between the corn and the storm clouds. Touch anything, or the ground, and the punt is down. Tap once and the next one is up.
  • Returners jump for the ball as it goes over, and later crows fly at you; a red arrow warns you first.
  • Hang time is your score. The gold dashed line is your best punt.
  • Kernels unlock new balls. The big ones against the corn and the clouds are worth five, and so is squeezing through a gold Coffin CORNer gate.
  • A helmet takes one hit for you.
  • Keep an eye on the paint on the grass as it goes by.
  • Now and then a positive word flashes in the sky, too quick to read. They are things like "you've got this" and "breathe", and nothing else.
  • A punt of five seconds or more spins the kernel slots when it comes down. Three ears of corn wins the jackpot, which grows until someone hits it.

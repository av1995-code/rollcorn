Tap when the arrow is on the gold to punt. Then tap to rise, and hold to float. You score 10 points for every second in the air, plus bonuses for corn, kicks through the uprights and close calls. You get three lives a game, and your score carries across them. A punt earns a spin on the slots at 150, 400 and 800 points. Down arrow, or a drag downward, dives. P pauses. Spins and catches give you a power-up. You hold one at a time: press the space bar, or tap its badge, to use it.

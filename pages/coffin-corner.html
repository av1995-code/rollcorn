    How it works

    • Tap, click or press the space bar to punt. Every tap after that lifts the ball. Stop tapping and it drops.
    • Fly between the corn and the storm clouds. Touch either one, or the ground, and the punt is down.
    • Returners jump for the ball as it goes over. Later on, crows fly at you; a red arrow warns you first.
    • Hang time is your score. It gets faster and tighter the longer you stay up, and the sun goes down.
    • Pick up kernels to unlock new balls. A gold Coffin CORNer gate is a tight squeeze worth five.
    • Grab a helmet and it takes one hit for you. Grab a magnet and kernels fly to the ball for a few seconds.
    • Squeeze past the corn or a cloud with almost no room and it counts as a close call, worth a kernel.
    • Beat a goal to earn kernels, and a harder one takes its place.
    • Collect the letters C, O, R and N. They carry over from punt to punt, and the fourth one sets off a popcorn frenzy: six seconds with nothing in your way and kernels everywhere.
    • The dashed lines are the next rank and your best punt. Fly through them.
    • Your first punt each day pays kernels, and more for every day in a row.

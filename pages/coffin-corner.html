Tap the field, press the button, or hit the space bar.

How it scores

  • Tap once to set the angle, and again to set the power.
  • Keep the ball up for 4.40 seconds and your coverage gets there first: no return.
  • Come down sooner and the returner brings it back. The shorter the hang time, the longer the return.
  • Stop it inside the 10 for a Coffin CORNer bonus. Inside the 5 is worth double.
  • Reach the end zone and it is a touchback: out to the 20, with no hang time bonus.

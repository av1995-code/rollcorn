Tap the field, press the button, or hit the space bar.

How it works

  • Tap once to set the angle, and again to set the power. The marker shows where the ball will come down.
  • Keep the ball up for 4.40 seconds and your coverage gets there first: no return.
  • Come down sooner and the returner brings it back. The shorter the hang time, the longer the return.
  • Stop it inside the 10 for a Coffin CORNer. String them together and every punt is worth more, up to five times.
  • A touchback, or a punt that leaves them outside the 20, costs an ear. Lose all three and the game is over.
  • Three Coffin CORNers in a row grows an ear back. The meters get faster the longer you last.
